    Where the design comes from

    Names
    Every control has a name that assistive technology can read: WCAG’s Name, Role, Value. Labels are tied to their fields, and diagram buttons named by their own words.
    Targets
    On a phone, a target is at least 24 by 24 CSS pixels: WCAG’s Target Size (Minimum).
    Text
    Text is at least 11 px, and at a contrast of at least 4.5:1, WCAG’s minimum for normal text.
    SC4S
    A source’s own ports are one variable per source and protocol, its ports comma-separated with no spaces. An env_file takes a comment only on a line of its own.

    Sources

    WCAG 2.2
    Name, Role, Value, Target Size (Minimum) and Contrast (Minimum).
    SC4S
    Sources, its unique listening ports; Configuration, TLS; and its entrypoint, the default ports.
    Docker
    docker container run, on --env-file.
