How complete a topology is, area by area; one sign-in followed from the person to the audit record, with the ways it fails; and groups of components saved and inserted in one step.     Where the design comes from

    Splunk’s rules, as Splunk gives them
    Three members for a highly available search head cluster, replication factor 3 and search factor 2, a Monitoring Console off the search heads, forwarder acknowledgement: each check links the page it rests on.
    Checked against Splunk
    A Splunk account’s sign-in, a wrong password, the lockout after five, an index and a capability the role lacks, and a session that timed out, on Splunk Enterprise 10.6. SAML, LDAP and MFA weren’t tried: no identity provider was available.
    Nothing leaves the page
    Saved patterns stay in this browser until downloaded; files are read in the tab.
