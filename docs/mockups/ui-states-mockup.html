States and focus

A visual audit of Build 238 asked whether people can tell what is clickable, what is selected, what belongs together and what needs attention; a review of every UI element asked what each one does with a mouse, a keyboard and a screen reader. Build 239 makes the 13 findings (F1 to F13) and the 11 items (U1 to U11) that came out of them, 22 changes where two overlap, and fixes a bug found on the way: a click in ten OV views showed OV-1. The pictures are 1440 px windows, Build 238 and the prototype; the markers name the finding or item.

    Where the design comes from

    Focus
    Keyboard focus is visible (Focus Visible), with a ring 3:1 or more against what is around it (Non-text Contrast), one style everywhere.
    States
    A chosen entry, card or view is shown by more than a fill: a 3:1 border or bar, and a state a screen reader reads (aria-current, aria-pressed).
    Boundaries
    A field’s edge, a card’s edge and a button’s edge are 3:1 or more against the ground (Non-text Contrast).
    Errors
    A refused value is described in words, under the field and tied to it (Error Identification), with a sign as well as a colour (Use of Color).
    Pointer and keyboard
    What acts on a click acts from the keyboard (Keyboard), and the pointer is kept for what acts. A modal dialog keeps focus and gives it back. Targets are 24 px or more, or have an equivalent that is (Target Size, Minimum).
