    The fixes in short

    Focus canvas
    The palette and the Inspector open in front of the full-screen canvas: z-index 105, between the canvas (100) and the focus bar (110). They start 8 px below the bar, wherever it ends, and stop above the bottom controls. Phones keep their sheet, which was already in front.
    The toolbar
    From 821 px it wraps: one row while the search box, the pipeline filter and the view switcher fit, then the switcher on a second row. The filter keeps its width. From 521 to 820 px the switcher’s buttons wrap.
    The capacity model
    From 821 to 1,080 px, server.conf goes under the other two.
    The Failure Lab
    Below 821 px the propagation map scrolls in its own box, not the page.
    The save label
    Build 140’s label reads the save status’s dirty class, so it says Saved after a save. Today it is hidden at every width.
    The help card
    “Shift+Space extend selection”.
    Unchanged
    At 1,320, 1,440 and 1,920 px every element in the seven main views lays out exactly as on Build 205. The Builder’s and OV’s own toolbars are untouched.
