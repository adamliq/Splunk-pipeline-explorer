Builder: one menu bar and one toolbar

From 761 px wide, the Builder gets one menu bar (File, Edit, View, Arrange, Validate, Analyze and Help) and one toolbar (undo and redo, zoom, + Add, the plane toggles, Find and the Validate status). They replace the two action rows, the dock’s Tools and Groups buttons, the canvas’s bottom bar and zoom, and the SOAR pill. Every scene is the real Builder with the prototype patch applied; the “Today” scene is Build 197 as shipped.

    The rules in short

    ≥ 761 px
    The menu bar and the toolbar replace the two rows, the dock buttons, the bottom bar, the zoom group and the SOAR pill. The header keeps its height, so the canvas keeps its size.
    Delegation
    Each menu item and toolbar button clicks the control it replaces. Those controls stay in the page, hidden, so their handlers, states and history labels are unchanged.
    Keyboard
    ← → move between menus, ↓ ↑ through the items, Esc closes. Ctrl Z, Ctrl Y, /, 0 and A work as before.
    < 1280 px
    Undo and Redo show icons only, the planes lose their swatches and the Validate status shortens. + Add opens the Components drawer.
    Focus canvas
    Unchanged: its own bar, bottom bar and zoom.
    ≤ 760 px
    Phones keep today’s Builder.
    Build 197 fixes
    When docked, the first-visit help card, its shortcuts and the minimap show again. At every desktop width the first fit keeps the topology above the help card, and Esc closes the open Tools or Groups panel.
