Vision
Every security-relevant event across our sites and cloud workloads is collected, trusted and searchable within a minute, so analysts detect, investigate and contain threats in hours, not days. Timeframe: Oct 2026 – Dec 2027, in three increments · Scope: Sydney and Melbourne sites, Windows estate, AWS workloads

What the vision means in numbers. Baselines come from the current platform; targets are due by the end of the increment shown.

Strategic drivers

  • Ransomware and credential theft are the top two threats in the risk register. Both start with signals already in our logs.
  • Privacy obligations: telemetry carries usernames and IP addresses, so it is PII and needs role-based access and set retention.
  • Workloads moving to AWS: Lambda and API sources are not onboarded today.
  • Licence spend grew 30% last year without a matching growth in detections.
Source: security strategy and risk register (example)

Desired effects

  • An analyst starts every investigation from one search across all sites and AWS.
  • A high-severity finding becomes a SOAR case with enrichment before anyone opens it.
  • Losing one site loses no indexed data and no search capability.
  • Only named roles can read PII indexes, and every read is audited.
