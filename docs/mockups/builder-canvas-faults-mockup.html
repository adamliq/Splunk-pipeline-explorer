Builder: two canvas faults

Two faults on the Builder canvas, both in Build 200. A card whose chips wrapped to a third row drew its container chip over its first stage row; now the chips keep to two rows, and the stage rows start below them. A container’s − collapse button covered the start of its name, so “Sydney” read “−dney”; now it sits at the top right. Every scene is the real Builder with the prototype block appended to Build 200; the “Today” scenes are Build 200 as shipped.

    The rules in short

    Cause, chips
    From 761 px a card is 224 px tall and its chip box 38 px, room for two rows. Nothing stopped a third row, or a chip’s second line, from running into the stage rows.
    Why not grow
    The layout places cards, containers, lanes and links on the 224 px height, so the chips keep to the two rows they have instead.
    Chips
    One line each, at most the card’s width, ending with … when longer.
    Region and zone
    Both count as at most half the row while the chips are placed, so the zone always fits beside the region. Then each grows to its own width, and the shorter gives the longer the rest of the row.
    Tooltips
    A chip or container name that ends with … shows its full text when you point at it. A tooltip set elsewhere, such as an S3 region’s, is kept.
    Cause, button
    .unifiedGroupBox button sets left: 8px on every button in a container, and it outranks .builderGroupCollapseButton, so the − sat at the left, on the name.
    The − button
    At the top right, 8 px in, level with the name. A name stops 48 px short of the container’s width, so it ends with … before the button.
    Unchanged
    Phones; zoomed out, where chips are hidden as before; card sizes and every position on the canvas.
