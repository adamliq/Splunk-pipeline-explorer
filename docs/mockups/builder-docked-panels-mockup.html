Builder: dock Components and the Inspector

From 1280 px wide, Components sits on the left, the canvas in the middle and the Inspector on the right, all visible at once, as in draw.io. Nothing covers the canvas, selecting a component no longer closes Components, and each column hides to a rail. Below 1280 px the panels stay drawers. Every scene is the real Builder with the prototype patch applied; the two “Today” scenes are Build 196 as shipped.

    The rules in short

    ≥ 1280 px
    Three columns: Components 280 px · canvas · Inspector 340 px (resizable from 300 px to 560 px, as long as the canvas keeps 580 px). Both open by default.
    Hide / rail
    “‹ Hide” and “Hide ›” collapse a column to a 40 px rail; the rail shows it again. Remembered per browser.
    Selection
    Selecting never closes Components and never reopens a hidden Inspector; “Inspect settings” does.
    Nothing selected
    The Inspector shows the topology: components, links by plane, Validate findings.
    Drawers
    Open over the canvas without closing the columns. The SOAR pill hides while one is open.
    Pin
    Removed from 761 px up, where it covered the canvas. Phones keep it.
    < 1280 px
    Drawers over the canvas, one at a time, as today. Phones unchanged. Focus canvas uses its own overlays.
