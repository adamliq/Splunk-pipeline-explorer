Scenario library packs

Failure, recovery and data-quality scenarios kept in packs: run together on the topology of the moment, opened in their simulator, saved from it, and carried as a file. Each scenario is worked out by its own simulator’s model; the library adds none.

    Where the design comes from

    The simulators’ own models
    A pack holds inputs only. Running it, each scenario goes through the Failure Lab’s, the Recovery simulation’s or the Data-quality simulation’s model, on a copy of that simulator’s settings, against the topology of the moment.
    The same figures in both places
    Every built-in scenario that runs on the default topology was opened from the library and its simulator read: 27 of 27 show the library’s figures.
    Nothing leaves the page
    Your packs stay in this browser until downloaded; pack files are read in the tab.
