Line jumps where links cross

D6 from the Build 212 recheck’s list of draw.io suggestions. Where a link’s horizontal run crosses another link’s vertical run, the horizontal one jumps over it with a small arc, on the canvas and in every export, and View › Line jumps turns them off. Every target scene is the real explorer with the prototype applied to Build 212; the “Today” scene is Build 212 as shipped.

Jumps where links crossWhere two links cross, nothing shows which way each goes: in the zones topology lines cross once in the horizontal layout, 4 times in the vertical and 6 times in Free place; its export of Free place has 10 crossingsEach link is drawn on its own, as straight runs with small rounded corners, so nothing looks at two links at once. Exports draw their own copyThe horizontal link of each crossing jumps over the vertical one with a small arc, on the canvas and in every export, with View › Line jumps to turn them off

    The design in short

    Jump
    An arc 10 px wide and 5 px high on the horizontal run, rising above it whichever way the link goes, as Visio draws them. The vertical link is unchanged.
    Crossings
    Only real crossings, clear of corners: more than 8 px from both runs’ ends, so an arc never runs into a corner or an arrowhead; that leaves 3 of the zones topology’s 11 flat. Links that share a trunk, meet end-on or join another don’t jump. Crossings closer than an arc share one wider arc.
    Where
    The canvas in every layout, at any zoom, on phones and touch screens; during a drag the arcs follow the routes at every frame. Export SVG and PNG, the presentation and the architecture pack.
    Switch
    View › Line jumps, on by default, kept in the browser. Off, everything is as on Build 212.
    Unchanged
    Labels and issue badges sit where Build 212 puts them. Clicking a link, selection and hover work as before.
