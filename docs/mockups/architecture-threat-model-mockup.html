How the Builder could generate this page

The diagram is the same topology drawn in DFD notation. Components become processes, sources and people become external entities, and queues, indexes and app repositories become data stores. Trust boundaries come from the Trust zone group containers and each component's security zone. A crossing is any relationship whose two ends sit in different boundaries, and its STRIDE threats are seeded from the relationship details the Builder already records.
