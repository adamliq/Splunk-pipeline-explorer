Collapsed containers, and redraws drawn right in their first frame

Faults 42 to 46 from the Build 215 recheck. Exports draw each collapsed container in its own place, a collapsed container’s box keeps its “Expand” pill clear of its text, and a redraw is right from its first frame: text at its final size before anything measures it, one Highlight row, the canvas at its height, and a phone’s collapsed links on their cards. Every target scene is the real explorer with the fixes applied to Build 215; the “Today” scenes are Build 215 as shipped.

    The fix in short

    Exports
    Each collapsed card in an export is numbered on its own, so each has its own place, and exports with collapsed types draw.
    Expand
    A collapsed container’s pill sits beside its first line, which wraps clear of it; the box is 120 px tall from the start.
    Text
    The polish runs as soon as the redraw’s task is done, so no code after it, and no frame, sees the smaller text.
    Group by
    One Highlight row; the canvas keeps its place through redraws, and its height follows its top in the same frame, so the Builder also opens where Fit puts the topology.
    Phone links
    A collapsed container’s links start and end 3 px off the cards as drawn, and are routed once.
    Unchanged
    The polish’s floors; Build 214’s second routing when a container’s box changes size; the settled Builder, once fitted; an export with one collapsed container.
