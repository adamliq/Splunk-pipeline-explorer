    The design in short

    Free place
    Every card starts with room below it before the next card in its column: 30 px for the Event data handle row that shows with the pointer on a card; on a touch screen, where the rows always show, their depth and 6 px more. Heights are read from the canvas: 224 px above 760 px, as tall as their content on a phone. A dropped card and add-next keep 30 px.
    Containers
    A container’s name clears whatever is above it in its columns: a card, its handle rows where they always show, or another container’s box. Its row and every row below move down together, in the horizontal and vertical layouts, on phones and in Free place’s starting positions.
    The box
    Reaches 18 px below its lowest card, as it does at its sides, whatever the card’s height.
    The name
    Drawn over links, still under cards, so a link through it doesn’t hide it or take its click.
    Matrix header
    A copy, fixed under the toolbar while the real header is scrolled away: at 83 px at 1,440 px, 139 at 1,024 and the top of the page on a phone. It follows the box’s sideways scroll and its column widths.
    Config view
    A stage button scrolls its row in below the header copy; on a phone, where the toolbar doesn’t stick, below the copy at the top of the screen.
    Unchanged
    With no containers every layout is as before; positions placed by hand in Free place stay; the horizontal and vertical layouts keep 24 px between rows; the real header is what screen readers read.
