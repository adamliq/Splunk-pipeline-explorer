Clicking a link’s label or issue badge

Item 34 from the Build 211 recheck’s list. A click on a link’s label selects the link and shows it in the Inspector, as a click on its line does, and a click on its issue badge selects the link and opens its findings. Every target scene is the real explorer with the five edits applied to Build 211; the “Today” scenes are Build 211 as shipped.

    The design in short

    Label
    A click selects the link, as a click on its line does: the Inspector shows it, opening over the canvas below 1,280 px, and the canvas draws the link as selected.
    Badge
    A click selects the link and opens its review findings beside the badge, with Open Review drawer. Below 1,280 px the Inspector stays closed so it doesn’t cover the findings; opening it shows the link.
    Drag
    A press on a label becomes a drag once the pointer has moved 3 px. Less is a click: the label stays put, and nothing goes into the undo history.
    Ids
    Additional data links (“data-rel-…”) are read as a click on the line reads them, so their labels select them too.
    Unchanged
    A drag once started, a click on a line, Enter on a focused badge, a card’s badge, and touch.
