Isolation, label and waypoint drags, the card badge and default ports

Eight Builder faults, fixed with eight in-place edits to Build 219. Five are from the Build 215 recheck’s open list: isolating a region or an environment (28), dragging a link’s label (35 and 36), a card’s issue badge (37), and the ports of syslog and search dispatch links (21). Three were found while testing those fixes: the same two drag faults in a link’s waypoint handle (50 and 51), and leader lines left for the labels of hidden links (52). Every target scene is the real explorer with the edits applied to Build 219; each “Today” scene is Build 219 as shipped, set up by the same steps.

    The fix in short

    Isolation
    No error. Only the links between the cards still shown are drawn, and the redraw runs to its end: the Find box, its options and Clear isolate stay where they are.
    Leaders
    A label hidden with its link, by isolation or by a collapsed container, gets no leader line.
    Label drags
    A label follows its whole drag, an additional data link’s too. One drag is one Undo step.
    Waypoint drags
    A waypoint stays where it is let go, and its link runs through it during the drag. An additional data link’s too.
    Card badge
    A click, or Enter, selects the card, outlines it, shows it in the Inspector and opens its findings. Below 1,280 px the Inspector is left closed, as for a link’s badge.
    Ports
    A new syslog link starts at 514, or 6514 where its protocol is TLS with no UDP; a new search dispatch link at 8089.
    Unchanged
    A click on a label; a press on a label’s issue badge; where labels may be placed; a port a link or a file already holds, 0 included.
