    The design in short

    Toolbar
    From 821 px, in every view but the Builder and OV, one row. The search box keeps at least 250 px and the filter its width; views that don’t fit go into More, in their order: eight at 1,300 px, five at 1,024, two at 821.
    More
    A menu under More. It takes the current view’s name when the current view is in it. Choosing a view returns focus to More; Esc or a click elsewhere closes it.
    Lanes and boxes
    A lane ends 18 px below its lowest card, a Component type box 10 px, whatever the card’s height, fitted again when a card changes size.
    Type headers
    Keep 6 px clear of the card above: rows move down, the group’s row and every row below it together. Drawn over links, under cards.
    Titles
    One line, ending with … when they don’t fit; hover shows the whole title.
    Labels
    Keep clear of containers’ names and collapse buttons and of type headers, as they do of cards. Where a link comes into a group’s top card from directly above, the gap above the header holds its label: 34 px.
    Badges
    A badge on a link’s midpoint that would sit under one of those controls, or on another label, moves along its link to the nearest clear spot.
    Unchanged
    With no Group by and no containers every card is where Build 210 puts it; from 1,310 px and at 820 px and below the toolbar is as before; the Builder and OV keep their own toolbars; Free place.
