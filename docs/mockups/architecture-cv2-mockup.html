Purpose

The capabilities in the CV-1 vision, broken down into the parts we can build, measure and assign. Each level-2 capability is part of its level-1 parent and has one measure with a current value, a target and the Splunk source the value comes from.

Capability measures

One measure per level-2 capability. Targets are due by the end of the increment shown (CV-1 roadmap).

IDCapabilityMeasureCurrentTargetByMeasured fromIn topology

Conditions the measures assume

  • Load: normal ingest 180 GB/day; incident peaks at 2× for up to 6 hours.
  • Sites: Sydney and Melbourne both up, except for the C3.3 and C4.3 measures, which are taken with one site down.
  • Search window: response times are for a 24-hour search across all security indexes.
  • Population: 1,850 Windows servers and workstations, 6 AWS accounts, 14 network sites.
