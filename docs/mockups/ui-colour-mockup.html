Colour and clarity

Every page of Build 237 was swept for its colours and its clarity: 86 page states at two widths, each status word’s colour, the headings, first paragraphs, abbreviations, sizes and capitals, and the component colours measured pair by pair. Build 238 makes the 13 changes that came out of it: eight on colour, from OV’s unfinished dark theme to one colour per tier and one status scale, and five on clarity, from Operate’s headings to a glossary of 96 abbreviations.

    Where the design comes from

    Colour
    Colour is never the only way something is shown: WCAG’s Use of Color. Each status word has a sign, and each component is named wherever it is coloured.
    Contrast
    Text at least 4.5:1 against its background (Contrast, Minimum); a component’s colour at least 3:1 against the ground (Non-text Contrast).
    Distance
    Any two component colours at least 15 apart for normal vision and 8 with red–green colour blindness simulated, in OKLab ΔE ×100.
    Headings
    A heading names what follows it (Headings and Labels), and the headings show the page’s structure (Info and Relationships).
    Abbreviations
    A way to find what an abbreviation stands for: WCAG’s Abbreviations, with the abbr element’s title.
