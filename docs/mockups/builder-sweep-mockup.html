    Where the design comes from

    One field, one job
    Print / present keeps its title, date and classification in its own state; View › Presentation keeps its on/off in another.
    Labels
    A label’s box fits its text at every zoom where it shows, its text in the middle; it keeps clear of cards, badges and, on a phone, the connection handles.
    Controls
    Two controls never overlap; every control has a name: WCAG’s Name, Role, Value.
    Text
    At least 11 px in the Builder’s panels, whatever is chosen or open, and at least 4.5:1: WCAG’s Contrast (Minimum).
