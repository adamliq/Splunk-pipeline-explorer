Export SVG as the canvas draws it

Faults 38 to 40 from the Build 213 recheck. Export SVG draws cards at the canvas’s size and every link on its canvas route, keeps its labels clear of each other and of the cards, and a collapsed container’s links are routed as other links are, with line jumps. Every target scene is the real explorer with the fixes applied to Build 213; the “Today” scenes are Build 213 as shipped.

    The fix in short

    Cards
    190 × 224 px, as on the canvas, showing the first two stages and the band at the foot; the export keeps its size.
    Routes
    Every link follows its canvas route: the same route function, given the canvas’s card boxes. A same-row Access link sits a third of the way down, as on the canvas.
    Labels
    On the link where clear of every card and label, else 4 px beside it, else left out with its link’s title still naming it. The export shows every label whose text the canvas shows.
    Collapsed
    A collapsed container’s links are routed as the canvas routes links, routed again when the container’s box changes size, and get line jumps.
    Unchanged
    The canvas, apart from a collapsed container’s links, and the export’s size, titles and legend.
