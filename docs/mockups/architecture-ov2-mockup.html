How the Builder could generate this page

An OV-2 is the same topology seen one level up: operational nodes instead of components, needlines instead of individual connections. The Builder can derive most of it by grouping components by tier and region, and each connection's details already hold transport, protection and timing. Two things per connection are new: what information is exchanged and which activity it supports.
