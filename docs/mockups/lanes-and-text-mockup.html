Lanes round every page’s cards, and canvas text at its final size

Faults 41 and 31 from the Build 214 recheck. Export SVG and the architecture pack draw each page’s Group by lanes round that page’s own cards, and the canvas draws its text at its final size from the start, so nothing grows a frame after a redraw. Every target scene is the real explorer with the fixes applied to Build 214; the “Today” scenes are Build 214 as shipped.

    What Build 214 does

    FaultWhat you seeWhyFix
    41. Lanes on a page of one part of the topology miss its cardsOn a page of one region, the lanes are where the whole topology’s cards would be; on every page, each card hangs 14 px below its lane; a collapsed container’s card is in no laneBuild 130 draws the lanes from the whole topology’s layout, put back once the page is made, and in the legend’s group, 30 px above the cardsThe lanes are worked out in the page’s own scope and drawn in the cards’ group, 6 px higher for their labels
    31.     The fix in short

    Lanes
    Export SVG’s and the architecture pack’s Group by lanes come from the page’s own layout and components and are drawn in the cards’ group, so every page’s cards are inside their own lanes, and each lane counts the cards in it.
    Collapsed
    A collapsed container’s card is in a lane, and counted there.
    Text
    Canvas text the polish raised a frame later is drawn at 11.5 px from the start; nothing on the canvas changes size after a redraw.
    Unchanged
    The settled canvas; the whole topology’s exports apart from their lanes; Azure cards’ and federated managed cards’ 9 and 10 px text; and the polish outside the canvas.
