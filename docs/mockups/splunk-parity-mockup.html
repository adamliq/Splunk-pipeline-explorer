    The rules in short

    Types
    A field’s value is a number where it reads as one, and text always. + adds two numbers and joins two strings; a number and text Splunk can see together rejects the whole expression.
    Conditions
    A comparison with a missing value has no answer, which NOT, AND, OR, XOR, if() and case() take as false. True and false are stored as 1 and 0.
    Numbers
    With a decimal point, a number keeps its precision: + and - the fewest places, * and / the fewest figures.
    Lookups
    One after another, in order of their names, each on what the earlier ones wrote.
    Extractions
    An empty value gives no field unless KEEP_EMPTY_VALS; quotes come off DELIMS values; MV_ADD keeps every value.
