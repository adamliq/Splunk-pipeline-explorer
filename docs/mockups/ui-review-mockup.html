Fixes and improvements from a review of every UI element

Every page and control of Build 236 was swept at three widths, used once, and looked at by eye. Build 237 makes the 19 changes that came out of it: five fixes, from queue bars that seldom showed their level to an OV header that named four views, ten for accessibility, from Inject’s unlabelled fields to 6.7 px diagram labels, and four improvements, from grouping OV’s 28 views by viewpoint to a phone header half its height.

    Where the design comes from

    Names
    A field is named by its label, tied to it: WCAG’s Info and Relationships and Name, Role, Value.
    Colour
    Colour is never the only way something is shown: WCAG’s Use of Color. Each tier mark carries a sign.
    Text
    At least 4.5:1 against its background (Contrast, Minimum), and 11 px or more, the Explorer’s own floor since Build 231.
    Targets
    On a phone, at least 24 by 24 CSS pixels: WCAG’s Target Size (Minimum).
    Focus
    Every control shows where focus is: WCAG’s Focus Visible.
    Motion
    Nothing moves on and on beside other content: WCAG’s Pause, Stop, Hide. A queue bar grows once.
